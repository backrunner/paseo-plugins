SubscriptionEvo queues messages for a chosen time or a delay after subscription quota recovers. It can also queue a continuation after a temporary provider failure and run a bounded goal loop across providers.

Open **Send later** beside the composer to enter a message and choose a time or quota recovery delay in a popover. **Queue · N** appears only while messages need attention. Messages can be edited, paused, reordered or canceled. **Schedule** authorizes only that message; **Queue only** waits for later approval. Automatic continuation, retries and quota resumption all start off.

Timed messages are submitted on the first scheduler check at or after their deadline, normally within 2.5 seconds plus connection time. They can steer an active turn and bypass messages still waiting for idle, quota, approval or a later time. Ordinary chat, closing the session and canceling a turn pause continuation while preserving explicitly authorized time and quota schedules. Explicit pauses, pending permissions, unavailable providers, provider changes and uncertain receipts still hold delivery. Idle and quota messages retain their idle checks and queue order.

Configure continuation per conversation under **Settings → Plugins → SubscriptionEvo → Continuation settings**. Open a goal with `/evo-goal`; opening the form does not start work. The composer popover is only for scheduling messages.

In a new conversation draft inside an existing workspace, open **Schedule start** to review the first message and model configuration. Choose a target workspace and a time. The daemon creates the conversation when it is due, even with the app closed. This schedules plain text and preserves the draft and its attachments in the composer. Open **Scheduled conversations** from the Command Center to edit, pause or withdraw a start and inspect its outcome.

Editing a queued message pauses it first. Save its text and timing in place with **Save paused** or **Save & schedule**; closing or discarding an edit leaves the original paused. **Withdraw** removes an unsent message and keeps its text in delivery history. Messages being sent or awaiting a receipt cannot be withdrawn.

This development version supports Paseo 0.16.2 and 0.17.4 and requires a local daemon endpoint on the host where the plugin runs. Connect it to that host's Paseo home on first use. It reads session state, recent history and quota reports, then sends to the existing conversation through the selected host. Queued text and settings are stored locally in the plugin's own directory. It uses Paseo's local connection credential; provider login tokens are not read or stored.

Quota recovery uses the target conversation's provider and account, requiring one fresh report with remaining capacity in every window. A user quota schedule starts its delay when available capacity is confirmed, including when quota has already recovered at the first check. It does not require enabling automatic continuation or quota resumption. Providers without usable quota reports can still use timed messages and goals. Unknown errors, authentication problems, permissions, manual input and ambiguous delivery outcomes pause automation. Goal completion is reported by the model with evidence and is not independently verified.

Every reported quota window is checked, including model-specific weekly limits such as Claude Fable. Plans with only weekly limits do not need a five-hour window. A previously reported window disappearing, missing or invalid data, expired login, authentication rejection or a failed query pauses the quota message with its reason. After fixing the problem, explicitly allow delivery to check again. Delivery refreshes quota once more before sending; an estimated reset time does not prove recovery.

Enter scheduled text in the popover above the composer. Evo keeps these messages in its own queue. For idle-based delivery, final checks cannot completely prevent another client from sending at the same moment. Turn off native Codex `/goal` before enabling Evo automation. `/evo-*` commands work only without attachments.

The development store retains at most 500 message records, 100 session policies and 8 MB of data. Reaching a limit stops new additions. Unsubmitted popover drafts remain in this client until it closes or the plugin reloads. Compilation and simulated behavior have been checked; real delivery and this popover's native presentation still need manual acceptance.
